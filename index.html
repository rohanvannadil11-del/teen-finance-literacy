💡 Why Financial Literacy Matters

Financial literacy is important because it teaches you how to manage money, avoid debt, and build a stable future. Without these skills, people often struggle with overspending, loans, and long-term financial stress.

  • Helps you avoid debt traps
  • Builds savings habits early
  • Improves decision-making
  • Prepares you for adulthood
  • Helps build wealth over time
