Welcome

Financial literacy is one of the most important life skills. This website provides free lessons, activities, and quizzes designed to help students understand money management, budgeting, investing, credit, and other essential financial topics.

Coursework

Lesson 1: Budgeting

Learn how to create and manage a budget.

Lesson 2: Saving

Understand emergency funds and savings goals.

Lesson 3: Banking

Checking accounts, savings accounts, and debit cards.

Lesson 4: Credit Scores

How credit works and why it matters.

Lesson 5: Investing

Stocks, ETFs, compound growth, and long-term investing.

Lesson 6: Taxes

Basic tax concepts every student should know.

About This Project

This website was created to provide accessible financial literacy education for students. Many young people graduate without learning practical money-management skills. The goal of this project is to make financial education simple, engaging, and available to everyone.
